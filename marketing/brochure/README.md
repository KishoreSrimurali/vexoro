# vexoro brochure

`vexoro-brochure.pdf`: tri-fold, A4 landscape (297 × 210 mm), two pages (page 1 outside, page 2 inside), three 99 mm panels per side.

- Outside (left → right): fold-in flap · back (contact, QR to vexoro.dev) · front cover
- Inside: packages (Starter, Business, Professional, Elite) · add-ons. Prices in OMR.

Printing: double-sided, flip on short edge, full colour, no scaling. Ask the print shop
for a 3 mm bleed if they need one; the background is full-bleed.

Edit `source/brochure.html` and re-export with `node source/render.js "$PWD/source"`.
